# Animation rules

## Handmade clips (hands, tools, materials, a process shot)

Camera is **completely static**, even when the original reference video moves the camera.
Only the hands, the tools and the material move. Copying a camera move into a handmade clip
is the most common reason one looks fake.

## Showcase clips (the product itself)

- **Case 1, the product is just sitting there:** slow handheld move in, with micro shake.
- **Case 2, there is action or movement in the original:** copy that movement 1:1.

## The motion sentence

Keep the motion description **short**. One precise sentence naming the actual objects beats
four vague ones. This is also the fix when a generation keeps failing: an overstuffed motion
description is the single most common cause. Rewrite shorter and simpler, do not add detail.

## Length by clip type

| Type | Typical | Hard range |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 4 to 6 s | 3 to 7 s |
| Showcase | 7 to 8 s | 6 to 10 s |
| Multi shot scene | 8 to 14 s | up to 15 s |

The student states the duration. Never guess a hook's duration, and never let the model pick.
