# The DO NOT block

Append to every Seedance prompt, as its own block, last.

```
DO NOT:
- NO camera movement (unless the clip brief explicitly asked for handheld micro-shake)
- NO music
- NO text overlay, NO captions, NO watermarks, NO logos burned into the frame
- NO cinematic bokeh, NO shallow depth of field, NO background blur
- NO glamour lighting, NO ring light, NO studio setup
- NO HDR, NO colour grading, NO film look
- NO AI-glossy face, NO plastic skin, NO beauty filter
- NO slow motion, NO speed ramping, NO time lapse
- The product is NOT modified, NOT restyled, NOT recoloured, NOT relabelled
- NO extra hands, NO extra fingers, NO extra people entering frame unless briefed
```

Two of these are the ones that actually fail in practice: text overlay creeping in, and the
product drifting off the reference. Put both in CRITICAL RULES as well, not just here.
