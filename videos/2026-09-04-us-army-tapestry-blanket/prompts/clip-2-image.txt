CLIP 2 - FIRST FRAME - IMAGE PROMPT
Run this in /brand, confirm the box design, then animate the result as clip 2.
This is the one frame the whole payoff rests on, which is why clip 2 takes the IMG route.

A photograph taken on an iPhone 12 inside a military surplus store. A metal retail shelf,
photographed from a standing customer's eye level, slightly from the left, so the shelf runs
away into the frame at a shallow angle.

On the shelf: six identical square retail boxes, stacked two rows high and three across,
all facing the camera, all the same design. Each box is olive drab with a black border of
small gold five-pointed stars. In the centre of each box, a flat two-colour illustration of
a rolled and folded blanket in olive and tan on a sunburst ground. Across the top of each
box in bold condensed black capitals: ARMYWORKS. Small gold star at each corner.

Around the boxes on the same shelving: coyote-brown combat boots below, tan and olive
tactical pouches above. White pegboard behind. White speckled tile floor.

Bright cool fluorescent tube lighting from directly overhead, straight down. Deep focus, the
boxes at the far end of the shelf as sharp as the near ones. No bokeh, no background blur.
Fine natural grain, no colour grading, vivid but natural colour.

Vertical 9:16.

DO NOT:
- NO shallow depth of field, NO bokeh
- NO studio lighting, NO ring light, NO product-photography look
- NO text other than ARMYWORKS on the boxes
- NO people, NO hands
- NO HDR, NO colour grading, NO film look
- The boxes are all identical. No variant designs, no different sizes.
